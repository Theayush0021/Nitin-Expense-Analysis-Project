# Nitin Expense Analysis & Savings Plan

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Nitin's six-month personal expense records (January to June) to understand spending patterns, identify major expense categories, and find opportunities to increase savings.

Nitin works as a Graphic Designer and earns ₹15,000 per month. He wants to purchase a scooter for his daily commute but has been struggling to save money.

The project uses Microsoft Excel for expense analysis, categorization, Pivot Tables, and data visualization to develop a practical savings strategy.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly and category-wise expenses.
- Identify the highest and lowest spending months.
- Understand essential and less-essential expenses.
- Analyze shopping, entertainment, food, and grocery spending.
- Compare monthly income with expenses.
- Identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary spending.
- Develop a realistic monthly savings plan for purchasing a scooter.

## 💡 Savings Strategy & Recommendations

The project suggests the following practical actions:

1. **Create a Scooter Savings Fund:** Transfer a fixed amount into a separate savings account immediately after receiving salary.
2. **Control Shopping Expenses:** Reduce unnecessary purchases and delay non-urgent shopping.
3. **Plan Entertainment:** Set a monthly entertainment budget and plan trips carefully.
4. **Reduce Convenience Food Spending:** Prepare weekly food plans and limit online food orders.
5. **Track Monthly Expenses:** Review spending patterns and investigate unusually high expenses.
